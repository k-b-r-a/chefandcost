Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026 • Application Package: com.chefandcost.app

At Chef&Cost (developed by k-b-r-a, repository recipetools), we believe that your culinary formulas, kitchen costing data, and personal information belong entirely to you. We are dedicated to maintaining the highest standards of data privacy and transparency, in full compliance with Google Play Store policies and international data protection standards (including GDPR and CCPA).

Key Principles:
  • Offline-First: Chef&Cost is fully functional without an internet connection or account.
  • Zero Advertisements: We do not serve ads, do not use the Google Advertising ID (AD_ID), and do not track users across other apps or websites.
  • Zero Data Selling: We never sell, monetize, or disclose your recipe or ingredient details to data brokers or third parties.

1. Information We Store Locally on Your Device

By default, all your data is stored locally in an isolated on-device SQLite database managed by the app:

  • Recipe Profiles: Recipe names, categories, descriptions, batch yields, portion counts, and preparation instructions.
  • Ingredients & Costs: Ingredient names, packaging sizes, purchase costs, and custom units of measurement.
  • Financial Calculations: Calculated recipe total cost, cost per portion, target profit margins, and suggested sale prices.
  • Kitchen Timers: Active and saved cooking timers and alert preferences.

This local database is sandboxed in your device's private storage and is never transmitted off your device unless you explicitly opt in to Cloud Sync.

2. Cloud Synchronization & Account Data (Optional)

If you choose to use our optional Cloud Sync feature to synchronize your recipes across multiple devices or platforms (Web & Android), we utilize Google Firebase services (Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication):

  • Account Authentication: When you register or sign in, we process your email address, encrypted password credentials, or Google Sign-In profile token via Firebase Authentication to create a unique User Identifier (UID).
  • Cloud Database Records: Your recipes, ingredients, custom units, and sync timestamps are stored in Google Cloud Firestore under a private path scoped specifically to your authenticated User ID (/users/{UID}/...).
  • Data Protection: Data in transit is secured using modern TLS 1.3 encryption, and data stored in Cloud Firestore is encrypted at rest using Google Cloud industry-standard security protocols.

3. Network Communications & External Services

Chef&Cost initiates network connections strictly for the following functional purposes:

  • Google Firebase (Firebase Auth & Cloud Firestore): To securely authenticate your account and sync recipe records when Cloud Sync is active.
  • GitHub Releases API (api.github.com): To dynamically fetch public release notes and application changelogs within the app. This connection is strictly read-only; no personal identifiers, device telemetry, or user information are transmitted to GitHub.

4. Device Permissions

Chef&Cost requests only the minimum necessary permissions required for app functionality:

  • Internet (INTERNET): Required for Cloud Sync with Firestore and fetching public release notes.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): On Android 13+, this permission allows the app to sound alarms and alert you when your cooking timers finish in the kitchen. Timer notifications run entirely on your device.
  • Vibration & Wake Lock (VIBRATE, WAKE_LOCK): To ensure timer alarms reliably wake the screen and vibrate when the alarm triggers.
  • No Sensitive Storage Permissions: Chef&Cost does not request or require MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or generic storage permissions. Shared recipe files (.chefy) are shared and opened securely using the standard Android Storage Access Framework and content providers.
  • No Advertising ID (AD_ID): Chef&Cost does not serve ads and explicitly removes the Google Advertising ID permission from its manifest.

5. Data Retention & Permanent Deletion

You have complete control over your data:

  • Local Data: You can clear local data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing the app storage via Android Settings.
  • Cloud Data & Account Deletion: In compliance with Google Play Store Account Deletion policies, you can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data directly within the app or via our web deletion portal.
