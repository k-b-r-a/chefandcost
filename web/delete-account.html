Delete Account & Cloud Data

Google Play Store Compliance • Package: com.chefandcost.app

In accordance with Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion Policy, users of Chef&Cost have the absolute right to request the permanent and irreversible deletion of their account and all associated cloud data.

Important: Deleting your account will immediately and permanently erase all cloud-synchronized recipes, ingredients, custom units, and authentication credentials from Google Cloud Firestore. This action cannot be reversed.

Summary of Data Subject to Deletion

Data Category Storage Location Action Upon Deletion Request
Authentication Account
Email, password credentials, and User ID (UID)		 Firebase Authentication Permanently deleted immediately.
Cloud Recipe Records
Recipe names, instructions, costs, batch sizes		 Google Cloud Firestore (/users/{UID}/recipes) All documents permanently deleted.
Cloud Ingredients & Units
Pantry ingredients, prices, packaging, units		 Google Cloud Firestore (/users/{UID}/ingredients) All documents permanently deleted.
Sync Metadata & Timestamps Google Cloud Firestore (/users/{UID}/sync_meta) Permanently deleted.
On-Device Local Database
SQLite database on your mobile device		 Local Device Sandbox Remains on your local device for offline access. To remove local records, uninstall Chef&Cost or clear app storage in your Android settings.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

You can perform an instant, automatic deletion of your account and cloud data directly inside the application:

Step-by-step instructions:

  1. Open the Chef&Cost app on your Android device or Web browser.
  2. Tap on Settings (or the Cloud Sync icon on the home screen).
  3. Select Cloud Sync.
  4. Ensure you are connected with the account you want to delete.
  5. At the bottom of the connected account card, tap "Eliminar cuenta y datos de la nube" (or "Delete account & cloud data").
  6. Enter your current account password in the confirmation dialog (if registered via email/password) to verify ownership.
  7. Tap "Eliminar definitivamente" ("Permanently delete").

✓ All Firestore data and authentication records are deleted immediately in real time.

Data Retention Policy

Chef&Cost does not retain residual backups of deleted accounts. Once deletion is completed, all database documents associated with your User ID in Google Cloud Firestore are completely and permanently destroyed. No personal data is retained for analytics or marketing purposes.
